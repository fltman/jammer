# Jämmer 0.1.0

The first release of Jämmer, a native Mac audio editor for podcasts and other speech. Free and open source (MIT). Requires macOS 27 or later on Apple silicon.

## What it does

- **Edit the transcript, and the audio follows.** Transcribe on your Mac (KB-Whisper for Swedish, Parakeet for 25 languages, or the Mac's own recognizer), then delete words, filler words and long pauses as text.
- **Restoration you can hear first.** Mains hum, clipping, clicks and noise, previewed against the original and undoable at any time. Mark a sound on the spectrogram with a box, a lasso or a brush and heal it.
- **Speakers found, levelled and cleaned.** Find who speaks when, one person per track or several on one; split them onto tracks; save a voice profile (on this Mac only, with consent) to name a speaker in later recordings.
- **Stems from a finished mix**: vocals, drums, bass and other, or speech, music and effects, on your Mac.
- **Loudness for where it's going**: presets for Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, EBU R 128 and ATSC A/85, measured to ITU-R BS.1770 and tested against the EBU's signals, with a true-peak limiter.
- **An assistant** that turns a request in English or Swedish into Jämmer's own commands, and an omnibar (⌘K) for every command.
- **Works with Flimmer and Glimmer**, and **agents can drive it** through a local API on 127.0.0.1 (the guide is served by the app).
- **Automation**, Audio Unit effects (other developers' plugins in their own process), a history tree that keeps every branch, and edits that never touch your files: untouched audio comes out bit for bit as it went in.

## Good to know

- Models are downloaded only when you ask, in Settings › Models or the first time a feature needs one. A sheet shows the size, publisher, source, version and license first.
- Paid services (OpenRouter, OpenAI, ElevenLabs) use your own keys, kept in the Keychain, and ask before each paid request.
- Speech made with a cloned voice is marked in the document and in every file exported with it, and the mark can't be removed.
- Not in this release: CLAP and VST 3 plugins, dialogue-gated loudness, ARA2, DDP and AAF.
