# Jämmer 0.5.1

A fix for something 0.5.0 could do. Requires macOS 27 or later on Apple silicon.

## Fixed

- **The omnibar could keep you waiting.** When what you type in the omnibar (⌘K) could be a command or a request, Jämmer asks Apple's model on your Mac which it is, and waits at most three seconds. In 0.5.0 that limit didn't hold when the model was slow to answer: the omnibar waited for it however long it took. Now it decides at the limit and sends the text to the assistant.
