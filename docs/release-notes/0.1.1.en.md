# Jämmer 0.1.1

A fix for something 0.1.0 did wrong. Please update.

## Fixed

- **A batch could send audio to OpenAI without asking.** In 0.1.0, a batch job that transcribed with OpenAI uploaded the audio and was charged to your OpenAI account without a question. This could happen only if you had added your own OpenAI key, and either used a batch recipe of your own with such a job or let an agent ask Jämmer for one. The built-in recipes have no paid job.
- Now the Batch window asks once before such a run. It names every paid job, says that the audio is uploaded to OpenAI, and says that Jämmer can't know the price. An agent's batch has to say both `allowPaid` and `allowUnknownPrice`, as every other paid call already did.

## Also in this version

- A clip that an edit renders again (a band cut, a spectral repair, de-bleed and the like) keeps its link to the Flimmer clip it came from, so Send to Flimmer sends it back as a new take of that clip.
- Jämmer pairs only with a Glimmer or Flimmer of exactly its own kind (a released app with released apps).
