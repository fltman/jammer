# The Emergency Biscuit Meeting

A four-person sample meeting about a biscuit that broke off in a coffee cup. The fictional speakers use Swedish, Danish, Norwegian Bokmål, and English.

| Speaker | Language | How Jämmer can find their name |
|---|---|---|
| Anders, meeting chair | Swedish | He introduces himself and states his role. |
| Anders, safety officer | Danish | He introduces himself and states his role. |
| Anders, biscuit inspector | Norwegian Bokmål | The Swedish chair introduces him immediately before he speaks. |
| Beth, risk officer | English | She introduces herself. |

Open the bundled Jämmer project to see the split tracks and transcripts. To reproduce the result yourself, open the mixed MP3 in Jämmer and choose **Audio › Find Speakers › 4 Speakers**, then **Audio › Split and Transcribe Speakers**. Install **Whisper small (multilingual)** in **Settings › Models** first. The independently detected languages and the speakers' introductions should distinguish the three Anders tracks. Use **File › Export Transcript** to save TXT, JSON, SRT, or WebVTT.

The dialogue in [dialogue.json](dialogue.json) is written for synthetic stock voices using ElevenLabs Eleven v4.
